Title: Quick way to add or strip VAT/GST from a price (the mistake I see most)
Body: When a client gives you a tax-inclusive total, don't subtract 20% of it — divide by 1.20. 1,200 -> 1,000 net + 200 tax. Check your own registration/rates with your tax authority.
