## Reddit (r/freelance — check self-promo rules; lead with the help)
Title: Put a number on what your slowest-paying client really costs you
Body: Late payment isn't only the wait: it's the financing cost of the delay plus the hours spent chasing. Formula: invoice x yearly cost of money x days late / 365, plus chasing hours x your hourly rate. Example with placeholder numbers: 2,000 invoice, 30 days late, 12% cost of money = ~19.73; 2 hours chasing at 50 = 100; ~119.73 per late invoice (~6%). Useful when deciding on shorter terms, a deposit or a late fee (check local rules and your contract). I made a free calculator if you want to plug in your own numbers: https://sendinvoicefree.com/tools/late-paying-client-cost-calculator/
