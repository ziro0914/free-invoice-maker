## Quora (answer to "How should I split payments on a long freelance project?")
For projects longer than a few weeks, most freelancers split the fee into 2-4 payments tied to deliverables rather than dates, e.g. 30% on signing, 40% when the first draft is delivered, 30% on final delivery. Put the milestone definitions, the client's review window and the payment terms in the contract before you start, and say that the next stage begins once the previous invoice is paid. (Check local rules on contracts/refunds.)
