## r/freelance (helpful-first; check sub self-promo rules)
How do you price monthly retainers? My rough method: hourly rate x reserved hours, then a small discount (5-15% is a common convention, not a rule) for guaranteed income, with extra hours billed at full rate and a written rollover policy. Example: 20h x $100 = $2,000, 10% off = $1,800 ($90 effective/hr). I put the math in a free calculator if useful: <link>. Curious what discount (or premium) you use.
