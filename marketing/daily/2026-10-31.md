## r/freelance (helpful-first; check sub self-promo rules)
Common day-rate mistake: dividing your old salary by 260 days. Not every day is billable (admin, sales, gaps, holidays). My method: (take-home goal + expenses) / (1 - tax set-aside) = revenue needed, then divide by realistic billable days. Example: 66,000 / 0.75 = 88,000; 46 weeks x 5 days x 70% = 161 days -> ~546.58/day (~68/hr at 8h). I put it in a free calculator if useful: <link>. How many billable days do you actually get?
