## 1. r/UKPersonalFinance / r/smallbusinessUK style answer (check sub rules; mention tool only if allowed; disclose "I built this")
If a UK business client pays you late, statutory interest on B2B invoices is simple interest at 8% above the Bank of England base rate, plus a fixed recovery sum that depends on the size of the debt (check the gov.uk table). Example: £2,000 at a 4% base rate = 12%, so 45 days late is about £29.59. I built a free calculator that does the sum (no sign-up): https://sendinvoicefree.com/tools/uk-late-payment-interest-calculator/ . Rules differ for consumers and contracts can change things, so check gov.uk.
