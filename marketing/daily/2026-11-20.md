## 1. Reddit (bartending / event-staffing communities; check self-promo rules, answer a question first)
Tip for event invoices: split labor (hours x bartenders), barback, setup/breakdown and supplies into separate lines, and show the deposit as a credit on the final bill. I made a free no-signup template laid out that way if useful: https://sendinvoicefree.com/invoice-template/bartender/

## 2. Reddit/forum (personal chef communities; check rules)
To avoid grocery disputes, keep your service fee and groceries (with receipts) on separate lines and agree reimbursement vs. built-in pricing up front. Free template with those lines, PDF export, no account: https://sendinvoicefree.com/invoice-template/personal-chef/
