## 3. Quora-style answer ("How do I invoice a carpentry job?")
List labor (hours x rate) and materials on separate lines, itemize big materials, and show any deposit as a credit so the balance is clear. Markup on materials is your call; put it in the quote first. Check local tax/licensing rules.
