## Quora — "What is a proforma invoice?"
A proforma invoice is a preview of a sale: it shows price, items and terms before the deal is final. It is not a demand for payment and usually isn't recorded in your books or given a number from your normal invoice sequence. The commercial invoice is the real bill, sent when the sale is complete or the goods ship. If you want a deposit, a clearly labelled deposit invoice is usually cleaner than a proforma. Customs and tax rules differ by country, so check with the relevant authority.
