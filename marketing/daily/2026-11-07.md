## 1. r/HVAC or r/hvacadvice-style (check self-promo rules first; lead with the tip)
Tip for small HVAC shops: put the diagnostic/service call fee on its own line, bill refrigerant by the pound on another, and state any after-hours surcharge before you show up. Disputes drop when the customer can see each line. I put a free no-signup template with those lines here if useful: https://sendinvoicefree.com/invoice-template/hvac-technician/

## 2. r/Roofing / contractor forums
Deposit on the invoice as a negative line ("Deposit applied") plus separate lines for tear-off, materials and labor makes the balance obvious. Deposit limits vary by state, so check your licensing board. Free template, no account: https://sendinvoicefree.com/invoice-template/roofer/
