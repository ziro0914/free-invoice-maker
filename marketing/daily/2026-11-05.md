## 2. Quora-style answer ("How do I stop clients asking for extra work on a fixed-price project?")
Put deliverables, exclusions and revision rounds in writing, and add a clause that out-of-scope work needs written approval with price and timing. When a request comes in, sort it: defect (fix free), clarification (already covered), or change (quote it). Example: 3,000 project estimated at 30h taking 42h pays ~71/h instead of 100; the 12 extra hours are worth 1,200. Check local contract rules.
