## Quora: "How do I price a freelance project for a profit margin?"
Divide your cost by (1 - target margin). $1,800 cost at 40% margin = $3,000, not $2,520 (cost x 1.4, which is only a ~28.6% margin). Include your own time in cost. I built a free calculator for this (disclosure: mine): https://sendinvoicefree.com/tools/profit-margin-vs-markup-calculator/
