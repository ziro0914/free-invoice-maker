## 2. Quora answer (question: "How should a freelancer calculate profit margin on a project?")
Margin = profit / price; markup = profit / cost. To hit a target margin, price = cost / (1 - margin). Example: cost $1,200, 35% margin -> $1,846.15. Include your own time in cost. I wrote this up with a calculator here (I built it): https://sendinvoicefree.com/guides/margin-vs-markup-for-freelancers/
