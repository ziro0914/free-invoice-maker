## 1. r/freelance-style answer (no link unless sub allows)
Going from a salary to freelancing, the common trap is dividing salary by 2,080 hours. You also need to replace benefits and employer-side payroll taxes, cover business costs, and only ~60-70% of your hours will be billable (your share may differ). Example: 70k salary +20% benefits +7.65% payroll tax = 89,355; +4k costs = 93,355; / 1,196 billable hours ≈ $78/h, about 2.3x the old hourly wage. Percentages vary by country, check local rules.
