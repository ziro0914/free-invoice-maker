## Reddit r/freelance (check sub rules on self-promo first; lead with the help)
Title: Late fees: agree them before the work starts, not after the invoice is overdue
Body: A fee added for the first time to an already-late invoice is hard to defend. What seems to work: put the rate and due date in your contract AND on every invoice, send a friendly reminder first, then apply it consistently. Common structures are simple monthly %, compounding, a flat fee, or "1.5%/month or $25, whichever is greater". Limits vary by state/country, so check local rules.
