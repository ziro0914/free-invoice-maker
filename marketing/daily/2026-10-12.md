## 1. Quora answer — "What's the difference between an invoice and a receipt?"
An invoice asks for payment; a receipt confirms it. The invoice goes out after the work is done and shows what is owed and the due date. The receipt goes out after the client has paid and shows a zero balance and the date paid. Many freelancers just send the original invoice back marked "PAID" with the payment date, which is usually fine for small jobs, but larger clients sometimes want a separate receipt. Keep the same invoice number on both. Rules vary by country, so check local tax requirements.
