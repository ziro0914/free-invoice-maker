## 1. Quora / answer sites (disclose "I wrote this")
Q: "Should I use Net 15 or Net 30 on freelance invoices?"
Net 15 means payment is due 15 days after the invoice date, Net 30 means 30. A common approach is to default to Net 15 for small or new clients and accept Net 30 when a larger client's policy requires it. Always write the actual due date, not just "Net 30". If you accept longer terms, consider a deposit or milestone invoices. Check your local rules on late-payment clauses.
