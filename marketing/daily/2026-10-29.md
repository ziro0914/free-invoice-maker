## Reddit-style answer (r/freelance: no link unless allowed; check sidebar)
Q: "How do I structure a monthly retainer?"
Price it as reserved hours x your normal rate, then decide if you give a small commitment discount. Write three things down: what's covered, what happens to unused hours (none / capped / expiry), and how extra hours are billed. Invoice in advance, state the period and the rollover rule on each invoice. Example: 20h x $100 = $2,000; 10% off = $1,800; 3 extra hours at $100 = $300, invoice $2,100.
