## 1. Quora answer — "How do I make my first invoice as a freelancer?"
Include: your name/business and the client's, a unique invoice number, issue date and due date, itemised work (description, quantity, rate), tax if you are required to charge it (check local rules), payment instructions and your terms (e.g. net 14). Agree scope and price in writing beforehand and send a PDF promptly with a clear subject line.
