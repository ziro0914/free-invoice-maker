Title: Your freelance rate isn't what you earn per hour
Body: If you bill $75/h but only 24 of your 40 working hours are billable, you earn $1,800 / 40 = $45/h. I wrote up the formula, a worked example, and how to back into a billing rate for a target effective rate. I built a free no-signup calculator alongside it: https://sendinvoicefree.com/tools/effective-hourly-rate-calculator/ (disclosure: it's my project).

## Draft B: Reddit answer-style (only where links are allowed; otherwise omit link)
"Track billable vs non-billable hours for a month. Effective rate = income / ALL hours. Example: $75/h x 24 billable hours = $1,800; over 40 hours that's $45/h. To get $50 effective at 60% billable you need to charge about $83/h. Rule of thumb, your mix will differ."
