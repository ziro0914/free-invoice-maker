## 2. Reddit answer-style (only if sub allows links; otherwise answer without a link; read sidebar first)
Question type: "I'm leaving my job to freelance, what rate should I charge?"
Draft: Don't divide your salary by 2,080. Add what your employer used to cover (benefits, payroll-tax share), your business costs, then divide by hours you can really bill (weeks worked x hours x billable share, often well under 100%). Example: 70k salary can land around 78/h instead of ~34/h with 20% benefits, 7.65% tax share, 4k costs, 6 weeks off, 65% billable. Those are my assumptions, not benchmarks.
