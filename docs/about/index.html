About Free Invoice Maker

Free Invoice Maker is a simple, free tool for freelancers, contractors and small businesses who need a professional invoice without signing up for accounting software.

Everything runs in your browser. We do not have a database of your invoices — there is nothing to hack or sell. Your business details are remembered only on your own device.

The site is supported by advertising and by partner links to tools we think are useful. That never affects the free generator.

Contact: via GitHub issues at github.com/ziro0914/free-invoice-maker.
