Invoicing tips for nannies

  • Record start and end times for each shift and keep the log with the invoice.
  • List overtime, extra children and holiday rates as separate lines with their multipliers.
  • Show reimbursements separately from pay and keep receipts.
  • Write the cancellation and guaranteed-hours terms on the invoice.

Typical line items

  • Regular childcare hours (per hour)
  • Overtime hours (per hour)
  • Additional child (per hour)
  • Reimbursement: outing admission and snacks

FAQ

Am I self-employed or an employee?

It depends on your arrangement and local rules. Some families treat nannies as household employees with payroll, others work with self-employed carers who invoice. Check your local tax authority's guidance.

How often should I invoice?

Weekly or every two weeks is common for regular care. Agree it upfront.

Can I bill for expenses?

Yes, if agreed. List each reimbursement as its own line and keep receipts.
