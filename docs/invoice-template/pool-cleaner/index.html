Invoicing tips for pool cleaners

  • Show the visit count and billing period on the monthly maintenance line.
  • List chemicals as their own lines with quantities so clients can see what was used.
  • Keep one-time repairs separate from recurring service.
  • Add the last water readings (chlorine, pH) in the notes if clients ask for proof of work.

Typical line items

  • Weekly pool maintenance, 4 visits (brush, skim, vacuum, filter check)
  • Chemicals: chlorine tablets (buckets used)
  • Algaecide treatment (one-time)
  • Skimmer basket replacement, parts and labor

FAQ

Should chemicals be included in the monthly fee?

Both approaches are common. A flat fee is simpler for clients, while separate chemical lines are more transparent when usage varies. Pick one and state it on your first invoice.

How do I bill a recurring client?

Send one invoice per month with the visit dates in the notes, and reuse the same template each time. See the invoice numbering guide.

Is sales tax charged on pool service?

It varies by state and sometimes applies only to chemicals and parts. Check local rules or ask an accountant.
