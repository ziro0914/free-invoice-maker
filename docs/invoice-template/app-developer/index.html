Invoicing tips for app developers

  • Tie each milestone to an acceptance event, not a date.
  • List change requests on their own lines so they can be approved separately.
  • Show third-party costs as pass-through lines, if your contract allows it.
  • Put maintenance hours and rate on a separate line from build work.

Typical line items

  • Milestone 1: design approved and prototype delivered (flat fee)
  • Milestone 2: core features complete on test build (flat fee)
  • Change request #1: add push notifications (hours)
  • App store developer account fee, passed through at cost
  • Maintenance plan, monthly (hours)

FAQ

Should I bill an app project hourly or by milestone?

Milestones suit defined builds, while hourly suits open-ended support and maintenance. Many developers combine both. Agree the model in your contract.

How do I invoice extra features?

List them as a separate change request with hours and rate, ideally approved in writing before work starts.

Who pays for app store fees and hosting?

It depends on your agreement. If you pay them upfront, list them as pass-through lines, and state any markup in the contract.
