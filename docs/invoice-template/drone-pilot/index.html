Invoicing tips for drone pilots

  • Separate capture, editing and licence into their own lines.
  • State licence scope: who, where and how long.
  • List add-ons such as rush delivery or mapping outputs separately.
  • Show travel and any permit costs as their own lines and keep receipts.

Typical line items

  • Aerial shoot, half day (flat fee)
  • Photo editing and retouching (per image)
  • Edited video, up to 2 minutes (flat fee)
  • Usage licence: web and print, 12 months (flat fee)
  • Travel (per mile)

FAQ

Should I charge for the usage licence separately?

Many pilots do, because the value to the client depends on how the images are used. Whatever you choose, put it in your contract and on the invoice.

Do I need to put my registration on the invoice?

Not always, but some clients ask for proof of registration and insurance. Requirements vary by country, so check your aviation authority.

How do I handle weather delays?

Agree a reschedule policy before the shoot and write it in your terms.
