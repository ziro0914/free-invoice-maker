Invoicing tips for data analysts

  • Name the task and tool on each line, for example 'SQL queries for churn report'.
  • Reference the agreement or statement of work on the invoice.
  • State how many revision rounds a fixed fee includes.
  • Put the service period on the invoice for retainer-style reporting.

Typical line items

  • Data cleaning and preparation (hours)
  • Dashboard build and handover (flat fee)
  • Monthly KPI report, analysis and commentary (flat fee)
  • Stakeholder walkthrough and training (hours)

FAQ

Hourly or fixed fee for analysis work?

Hourly suits exploratory work with uncertain effort. Fixed fees suit defined deliverables with clear scope and revision limits.

Do I charge sales tax on data analysis?

It depends on your location and the client's. Rules vary by state, country and service type, so check local rules or ask an accountant.

What should I attach to the invoice?

Often a short summary of work done or the deliverable links, so the client can match the invoice to approved work.
