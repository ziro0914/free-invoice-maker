Invoicing tips for voice actors

  • Keep session fee and usage fee on separate lines.
  • Write media, territory and term next to the usage line.
  • State how many revision rounds are included and price extra ones.
  • Quote any renewal or buyout terms in your contract and reference them on the invoice.

Typical line items

  • Recording session, up to 1 hour (flat fee)
  • Additional finished minutes (per minute)
  • Pickups and corrections (flat fee)
  • Usage: web and social, 12 months (flat fee)
  • Rush delivery within 24 hours (flat fee)

FAQ

What is the difference between usage and a buyout?

A usage fee covers specific media for a set term, while a buyout is usually a one-time payment for broader rights. Define the one you mean in your contract.

Can I charge for pickups?

Yes, if agreed in advance. List them as their own line, or say how many are included.

When should I invoice?

Commonly on delivery of the final files, or with a deposit up front for longer projects.
