Invoicing tips for hair stylists

  • Use one line per service and one per retail product.
  • Keep booth rent and commission splits off the client's invoice; track them in your own records.
  • Show tips as their own line so they are easy to log at tax time.
  • Retail products may be taxed differently from services in some places, so check local rules.

FAQ

Should tips go on the invoice?

Many stylists add tips as a separate line below the services so they are easy to track. Check local rules on how tips are reported.

Do I charge sales tax on hair services?

It depends on your state, province or country. Retail products are often treated differently from services. Check local rules or ask an accountant.

Can I use this if I rent a booth?

Yes, for invoicing your own clients. Keep your booth rent separate, and ask an accountant how your arrangement is treated for tax.
