Invoicing tips for moving companies

  • Show crew size, hours and the hourly rate so the labor line can be checked.
  • Note the start and end times, and how breaks and travel time were counted.
  • List the truck or travel fee separately from labor.
  • Itemize packing materials and surcharges such as stairs, long carries or bulky items.
  • Show any deposit as a credit against the total.

Typical line items

  • Crew labor: 3 movers x 5 hours (15 labor hours)
  • Truck and travel fee
  • Packing materials (boxes, tape, wrap)
  • Stair carry surcharge
  • Deposit received (credit)

FAQ

Should I bill by the hour or a flat rate?

Both are common. Hourly suits local moves with uncertain scope; flat rates suit well-defined jobs. Whichever you use, state it on the quote and the invoice.

How do I handle travel time?

Some movers bill travel from the yard and back as its own line, others use a flat truck fee. Agree the method before the move and show it clearly.

Do I need special licensing or insurance details on the invoice?

Requirements differ by state and for interstate moves. Check with your state regulator and federal rules, and consider showing your license and coverage details.
