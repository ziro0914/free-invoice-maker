Invoicing tips for wedding photographers

  • Show the deposit as its own negative line so the balance due is obvious.
  • Match package names, hours and add-ons to your signed contract.
  • List overtime, second shooter, travel and rush editing as separate lines.
  • Set the balance due date before the wedding if your contract allows it, and state it in the payment terms.

FAQ

How should I invoice a wedding deposit?

Many photographers send a first invoice at booking for the deposit, then a second for the balance. Alternatively, issue one invoice with the deposit shown as a credit line. Whether the deposit is refundable should be spelled out in your contract, and local rules on deposits can vary, so check them.

When is the balance usually due?

Common arrangements are some weeks before the wedding or on delivery of the photos, but it is whatever you agree in your contract. Put the date on the invoice.

Do I charge tax on wedding photography?

It depends on your location and on whether you deliver digital files, prints or both. Rules differ between regions, so check with your local tax authority or an accountant.
