Invoicing tips for tattoo artists

  • Show the deposit as a negative line so the client sees it credited against the total.
  • Write your touch-up window and conditions in the studio policy and refer to it on the invoice.
  • Itemize design time separately from tattooing time.
  • Add aftercare items as their own line if you charge for them.

Typical line items

  • Custom design and consultation (flat fee)
  • Tattoo session (hours)
  • Booking deposit applied (paid earlier)
  • Touch-up within 60 days of healing (per studio policy)

FAQ

How should I show a booking deposit on the invoice?

List it as a credit (negative amount) labelled 'Booking deposit applied'. Keep your refund and rescheduling policy in writing and shared before booking.

Should free touch-ups appear on the invoice?

A zero-cost line can remind clients of the policy. Specify the time window and conditions in your written studio policy.

Do I charge sales tax on tattoos?

It depends on your state, province or country, and rules differ. Check local rules or ask an accountant.
