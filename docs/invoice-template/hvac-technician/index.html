Invoicing tips for hvac technicians

  • Put the service call or diagnostic fee on its own line.
  • List parts with name, quantity and unit price; add model or serial numbers where useful.
  • Bill refrigerant by the pound as a separate line.
  • State any emergency or after-hours surcharge before you arrive, and note warranty terms on the invoice.

Typical line items

  • Service call / diagnostic fee
  • Labor (hours): replace capacitor, test system
  • Part: dual run capacitor (model on request)
  • Refrigerant (lbs)

FAQ

Should I credit the diagnostic fee if the customer approves the repair?

Some companies do and some don't. Decide your policy, tell the customer in the quote, and show any credit as a negative line.

How do I invoice a maintenance contract?

Bill the agreed visit or monthly fee as one line and describe the covered visits. See the retainer guide for recurring billing ideas.

Do I charge tax on parts and labor?

It depends on your location and the type of work. Check local rules or ask an accountant.
