How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Add the take-home pay you want to your yearly business costs, gross it up for tax, then divide by the hours you can actually bill in a year: weeks worked × hours per week × your billable share.

Why is the billable share so low?

Most freelancers spend 30-40% of their time on sales, admin, invoicing and learning. Those hours earn nothing directly, so your rate on billable hours has to cover them.
