How do I convert a salary to a freelance hourly rate?

Add the cost of what an employer used to cover (benefits, payroll taxes) and your business costs to the salary, then divide by the hours you can actually bill. For example, 70,000 x (1 + 20% + 7.65%) = 89,355; plus 4,000 of costs is 93,355. With 46 weeks x 40 hours x 65% billable = 1,196 hours, the rate is about 78.06 per hour, around 2.3 times a 33.65 salary hourly wage (70,000 / 2,080).

Are the benefit and tax percentages realistic?

They are placeholders, not statistics. Benefit value, payroll tax and income tax differ by country, employer and personal situation, and the tax portion here is not income tax. Check local rules or ask a tax professional, and replace the defaults with your own figures.
