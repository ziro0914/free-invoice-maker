How much should a freelancer keep in an emergency fund?

Many personal finance sources suggest 3 to 6 months of essential expenses, and some freelancer-focused sources suggest 6 to 12 months because income is less predictable. Add your essential personal and business costs, then multiply by the months you want. For example, (3,000 + 500) x 6 = 21,000.

How long will it take to build the fund?

Subtract what you have saved from the target, then divide by what you can add each month. With 5,000 saved against 21,000, the gap is 16,000; at 800 a month that is 20 months. Months of cover are a rule of thumb, not a rule, so adjust for other earners, insurance and how steady your clients are. Set tax money aside separately; this is general information, not financial advice.
