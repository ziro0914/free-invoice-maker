What is the difference between margin and markup?

Markup is profit divided by cost; margin is profit divided by the selling price. The same sale gives a bigger markup than margin. For example, a $1,800 cost sold at $3,000 is a 40% margin but a 66.7% markup.

How do I price a job for a target margin?

Divide your cost by 1 minus the margin, for example $1,800 / (1 - 0.40) = $3,000. Multiplying the cost by 1.40 would give $2,520, which is only a 28.6% margin. Remember to include your own time in the cost.
