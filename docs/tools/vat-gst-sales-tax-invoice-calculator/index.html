How do I add VAT, GST or sales tax to an invoice?

Multiply the net amount by the tax rate to get the tax, then add it to the net amount. For example, 20% on 1,000 is 200, so the invoice total is 1,200.

How do I remove tax from a total that already includes it?

Divide the tax-inclusive total by 1 plus the rate. For example, 1,200 divided by 1.20 is 1,000 net, so 200 of it is tax. Do not just subtract 20% of 1,200, which would give the wrong answer. Whether you must charge tax at all depends on your location and registration, so check with your tax authority.
