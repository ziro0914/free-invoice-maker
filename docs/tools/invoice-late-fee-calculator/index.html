How do you calculate a late fee on an invoice?

For a simple monthly fee, multiply the invoice amount by the monthly rate and by the months overdue. For example, 1.5% a month on $5,000 is $75 a month, or $225 after three months. With compounding, each month the fee is charged on the growing balance.

Can I charge a late fee on any invoice?

Only if your agreement or invoice terms say so, and many places cap or regulate late fees and interest. State the rate and due date on the invoice up front and check your local rules or a professional before charging.
