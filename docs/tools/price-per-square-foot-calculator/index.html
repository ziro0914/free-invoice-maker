How do I calculate a price per square foot quote?

Multiply length by width to get the area, multiply the area by your rate per square foot, then add materials. For example, a 30 x 20 ft driveway is 600 sq ft; at 0.25 per sq ft that is 150 of labor, plus 15 of materials, so 165 in total.

Why add a minimum charge?

Small jobs still cost you travel, setup and cleanup time, so a per-square-foot rate alone can leave you underpaid. A minimum charge covers that. The default numbers here are placeholders, not market rates; rates vary by region, surface and condition, so check your own costs and local prices.
