How do I calculate a monthly retainer?

Multiply your standard hourly rate by the hours the client reserves each month, then decide whether to give a small commitment discount. For example, 20 hours at $100 is $2,000; with a 10% discount the retainer is $1,800, an effective $90 per hour.

Should a retainer be cheaper than my hourly rate?

Practices differ. Some freelancers give a modest discount, often described as roughly 5-15%, in return for guaranteed income, while others charge a premium for reserving their availability. Bill hours beyond the commitment at your full rate and state in writing whether unused hours roll over.
