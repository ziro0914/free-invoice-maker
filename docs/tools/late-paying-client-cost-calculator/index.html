How do I calculate what a late-paying client costs me?

Add two things: the financing cost of waiting (invoice amount x your yearly cost of money x days late / 365) and the value of the time you spend chasing (hours x your hourly rate). For example, 2,000 at 12% for 30 days is about 19.73, plus 2 hours at 50 is 100, so about 119.73 per late invoice, or about 718.36 a year at 6 late invoices.

Is it worth charging late fees or asking for a deposit?

If slow payers cost you around 6% of each invoice in this example, shorter terms, a deposit or a late fee can be easier to justify. The 12% cost of money and the 2 hours of chasing are placeholders, not benchmarks; use your own figures. Late fees and interest are governed by local rules and your contract, so check them before you add one.
