How many clients do I need to hit my income goal?

Add your desired take-home pay to your business costs, gross it up for tax, then divide by what an average client pays you per year and round up. For example, (80,000 + 6,000) / (1 - 25%) = about 114,667 of revenue; at 8,000 per client that is 14.33, so plan for 15 clients.

How many proposals do I need to send?

Divide the clients you need by your win rate. At a 25% win rate, 15 clients means about 60 proposals a year, roughly 5 a month. The 25% default is a placeholder, not a benchmark; track your own results, since win rates vary widely by niche and lead source. Also plan for clients who leave, so aim a little above the number shown. Tax is an estimate only; check local rules.
