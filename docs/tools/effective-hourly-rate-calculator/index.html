How do I calculate my effective hourly rate as a freelancer?

Subtract business costs from revenue, then divide by every hour you worked, billable or not. For example, 6,000 revenue minus 600 costs is 5,400; with 90 billable and 40 unpaid hours that is 130 hours, so the effective rate is about 41.54 per hour, even though revenue per billable hour is 66.67.

Should I include tax in the calculation?

This calculator stops before income tax so you can compare it with your target rate. Tax rules differ by country and situation, so check local rules or ask a tax professional before treating the result as take-home pay. Time-tracking tools and your own logs give better inputs than guesses.
