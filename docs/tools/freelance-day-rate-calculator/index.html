How do I calculate a freelance day rate?

Add your take-home goal and business expenses, gross it up for tax, then divide by the days you can realistically bill. For example, 60,000 + 6,000 = 66,000; at a 25% set-aside that is 88,000 of revenue. With 46 working weeks x 5 days x 70% billable = 161 days, the day rate is about 546.58.

Why not just divide by every working day?

Not every working day is billable: admin, sales, learning and gaps between clients take time. Using a billable share such as 60-80% (your own figure may differ) stops you underpricing. The tax set-aside here is only a placeholder, so confirm your real rate with a tax professional.
